# Sahva — Product Brief

**Tagline:** Speak naturally. Understand everyone. Leave with clarity.

## One-line pitch
A two-person multilingual audio room in which people speak their own language, hear translated speech from others, review the actual original recordings and leave with one shared record of decisions, tasks and deadlines — displayed in each listener's language.

## Problem
People collaborating across Indian languages or across India and global markets face three compounding problems: translation friction during calls; no reliable trace back to what a speaker actually said; and meetings that end without shared, actionable understanding. Generic transcript summaries can turn suggestions into commitments.

## Product difference
Not a translation widget, not yet a replacement for full-duplex Zoom/Meet. Sahva is an **audio-first conversation workspace** with per-turn evidence, language preferences and a common action record. Context can include a glossary of names/products, and corrections are visible, attributable and reversible.

## Primary buildathon user
Two coworkers or business partners who do not share a fluent language. Demo pair: Telugu-speaking operations colleague + English-speaking manager, discussing dispatch and a deadline. The same workflow also supports exporters, suppliers and service teams. No industry hardcoding.

## P0 user journey
1. Room owner creates a room and consents to recording/AI processing.
2. They share an invite URL. A second person chooses a name and listening language and consents.
3. Both see who's present. One holds/taps Record and speaks in a <=25s turn; a visible record/processing indicator is shown.
4. Sahva stores original audio, returns an original transcript and translated text/audio for the other participant.
5. Recipient sees the new turn, can play translated audio, reveal original/translated text, and play source audio.
6. Participants swap roles for multiple turns; a visible timeline remains available.
7. Owner ends the meeting; Sahva generates a source-linked, language-neutral meeting record.
8. Each participant views the same facts localized into their preferred language, with action statuses (agreed/proposed/unresolved), people and deadlines.

## Signature demo moment
A speaker says in Telugu: “Ramesh will dispatch 20 cartons on Monday.” The English listener hears the translated speech. They respond in English with an explicit acceptance plus a request to confirm pickup time. At the end, both see the same action item in Telugu/English, attributed to the correct person, and an unresolved pickup-time question — each linked to the source turn(s).

## Measure success, not hype
- Two independently connected browsers exchange at least 4 real spoken turns (2 each).
- Source and translated audio both replay after refresh during room retention.
- Transcript toggle correctly hides or shows text without hiding audio.
- End-of-room report renders in Telugu and English with consistent underlying IDs/statuses.
- No invented deadline/owner in intentionally ambiguous test utterance.
- Every action/decision opens its supporting turn(s).

## Not built today
PSTN, WhatsApp, video, simultaneous continuous interpretation, voice cloning, production billing, automated calendar sending, guarantees of legal agreement, unrestricted multi-party calls.
